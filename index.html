Prototype assumptions & model

All numbers are illustrative teaching assumptions, not empirical estimates. Each style has uncertain demand around 120 units. Variety raises demand per style slightly. A fixed seed makes restarts comparable. Half of demand arrives before replenishment; the remainder is revealed through a noisy signal. Quick sourcing costs €16 versus €12 per unit and produces 7 versus 5 kg CO₂e per unit. Durable quality adds €6 and 2 kg CO₂e, with 40 uses per item versus 15. Each style costs €150 to develop. Leftover disposal adds 1 kg CO₂e per unit. The use index is sold units × assumed uses; it is not observed behavior. Transport, returns, labor, recycling, consumer substitution and competitive effects are omitted.
